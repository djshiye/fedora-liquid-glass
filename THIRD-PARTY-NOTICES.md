# Third-party notices

## AndroidLiquidGlass (Backdrop)

The glass lens in the GNOME Shell extension
(`liquidglass/data/extension/liquid-glass@djshiye.github.io/glass.js`) and in the app's preview
(`liquidglass/gui/qml/LiquidGlass/shaders/glass.frag`) is ported from Kyant's
[AndroidLiquidGlass](https://github.com/Kyant0/AndroidLiquidGlass): the rounded-rectangle
distance field and its gradient, the circular edge profile, the depth pull and the seven-band
dispersion. It was rewritten from AGSL to GLSL and changed to fit GNOME Shell and Qt Quick.
